My Wish

Teruntuk,
Siti, Quru, and Mun ♥

I know this might be a little late, but I just want to wish all of you a very Happy Graduation! Tahniah korang sebab dah berjaya habiskan perjalanan degree selama empat tahun ni. I know it wasn’t easy, and honestly, sometimes I think kita salah pilih course HAHAHA. Maybe this wasn’t really the course we expected, but despite all the challenges, you guys made it through. And I’m genuinely proud of all of us!

First of all, I just want to say thank you for being my friends since day one. I still remember how we first got to know each other, duduk satu row masa kelas Dr.Salwani kut yang Programming Techniques. Who would have thought that the friendship would grow this far? And I think our bond became even stronger after we joined PALAPES.

Through PALAPES, we’ve been through so much together. Ada gaduh, ada manis, ada pahit, ada busuk, ada wangi HAHAHA. We’ve seen different sides of each other, gone through difficult moments and created so many memories along the way. But one thing I’m really thankful for is that no matter what happened, you guys never made me feel left out. Thank you for accepting me, putting up with my perangai and being the kind of friends I could always count on.

I’m sorry if throughout our friendship, ada kata-kata, perbuatan or gurauan saya yang pernah menyakitkan hati korang, sama ada saya sedar atau tak. I hope there’s no hard feelings between us.

As we move on to a new chapter in life, I genuinely pray that Allah will always ease our affairs, open the best opportunities for us and grant us success in everything we do, dunia dan akhirat. Semoga apa yang kita impikan selama ni satu per satu tercapai.

Thank you for being part of my university journey. I’m really grateful that our paths crossed, and I hope this isn’t where our friendship ends. Lepas ni mungkin masing-masing akan sibuk dengan kehidupan sendiri, but please don’t be a stranger.

So, see you guys when I see you. Take care, keep in touch and don’t forget me okay! Love you guys! 🥹❤️

With Love,
Insyi ♥
